Is Your Older Car Costing You in Unnecessary Coverage?

Example: $2,000 car with $500 deductible = max payout of $1,500. If you're paying $600/year for full coverage, you're losing money. Liability-only coverage could cut your premium in half while still keeping you legal and protected from lawsuits.
